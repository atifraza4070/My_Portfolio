About My Professional Journey

Atif Raza

I am an Operations Executive and Team Lead with 3+ years of experience at OpenPort (Private) Limited, supporting logistics and transportation operations for Bulleh Shah Packaging (Packages Group). My experience covers shipment planning, dispatch coordination, fleet tracking, transporter and customer coordination, delivery performance, documentation, and operational issue resolution.

I specialize in turning logistics and transportation data into practical business insights through Microsoft Excel, Power BI dashboards, KPI tracking, and TMS-based monitoring. I work closely with transporters, customers, dispatch teams, and internal stakeholders to improve shipment visibility, monitor vehicle movement, track turnaround time, and support day-to-day operational decisions.

Alongside my professional experience, I am pursuing an Executive MBA in Marketing at the University of the Punjab and continuously developing my skills in Advanced Excel, Power BI, SQL, Python, SAP, Digital Marketing, and AI-based business tools. I also have professional experience in Adobe InDesign and book formatting, with 300+ book projects completed for platforms including Amazon KDP and IngramSpark.
